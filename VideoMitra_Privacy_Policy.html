VideoMitra Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

VideoMitra respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use the VideoMitra mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, VideoMitra may collect information such as your account information, user ID, videos you upload, video titles, comments, likes, and basic information required to provide app functionality.

2. Account and Authentication

VideoMitra may use Firebase Authentication to allow users to create an account and sign in. Authentication information is handled by the relevant service provider according to its privacy practices.

3. Videos and User Content

When you upload a video, the video and related information may be stored or processed by services used by VideoMitra to provide video-upload and playback functionality. You should only upload content that you have the right to share.

4. Likes and Comments

When you like or comment on a video, information necessary to provide these features may be stored in the application's database.

5. How We Use Information

  • To provide and operate VideoMitra.
  • To authenticate users and maintain accounts.
  • To display videos, likes, and comments.
  • To improve app functionality and security.
  • To respond to support requests.

6. Third-Party Services

VideoMitra may use third-party services such as Firebase and video hosting or other infrastructure providers. These services may process information as necessary to provide their services. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information used by the application. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. If you want to request deletion of your account or personal information, contact us using the email below.

9. Children's Privacy

VideoMitra is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law to use the service without appropriate consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
